📊 Methodology: Understanding "Presence" vs "Win Rate"

✓ Presence Rate

Measures if a brand is mentioned in AI responses: • Brand appears in the text or citations
• Acknowledged as offering the service
• Listed among options available
Example: "Hims also offers ED treatment..."

🏆 Win Rate

Measures if a brand is recommended as best: • Positioned as the top choice
• Receives most favorable framing
• Highlighted for advantages over others
Example: "Roman stands out as the leader..."

Key Insight: High presence with low win rate indicates brand awareness without preference - consumers know you exist but AI doesn't recommend you over competitors.
