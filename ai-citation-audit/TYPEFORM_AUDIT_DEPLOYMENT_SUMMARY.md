## 💼 Value Proposition for Corporate Communications Professionals

### 🎯 Core Value: Strategic PR Intelligence at Scale

Our AI Citation Audit tool transforms how corporate communications teams identify and capitalize on media opportunities by providing:

1. **Automated Competitive Intelligence**
   - Track competitor mentions across AI platforms in real-time
   - Identify gaps in your brand's AI visibility
   - Benchmark against industry leaders

2. **PR Opportunity Scoring**
   - Quantified opportunities (0-100 score) for media outreach
   - Prioritized list of high-value outlets for engagement
   - Data-driven PR strategy recommendations

3. **Media Outlet Classification**
   - Tier 1: National media (WSJ, Forbes, NYT)
   - Tier 2: Trade publications
   - Tier 3: Regional/niche outlets
   - Influence scores for each outlet
